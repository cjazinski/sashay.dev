FAQ

Does Tally send my activity anywhere?

No. The tracker writes a local JSON file under your Application Support folder. Tally makes zero network connections — the same promise every app in this portfolio keeps.

What about sensitive windows?

1Password, Keychain Access, SecurityAgent, and security-related System Settings panes are recorded with no title, under a "Private" bucket. The window title is never read for those apps.

How is this different from Screen Time?

Screen Time is buried in System Settings, syncs through iCloud, and offers no categories, rules, focus sessions, or export. Tally is menu-bar-first, local-only, and built for people who work in apps all day.
