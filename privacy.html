Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Jazinski Inc. · sashay.dev

The short version

Sashay makes zero network connections. It has no analytics, no ads, no trackers, no accounts, and no servers. We cannot see your usage, because nothing is ever sent anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings (intervals, schedules, preferences) — stored locally in macOS user preferences.
  • Your stats (moves and distance counters) — stored locally, never transmitted.
  • Your license key, if you purchase Pro — stored locally and verified offline against a public key inside the app.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe shares your email address and payment confirmation with us so a license key can be delivered; that address is used only for your receipt, key delivery, and support, and is never sold or shared further.

What we do not do

  • No collection of personal data beyond the email you provide at checkout.
  • No tracking, fingerprinting, or profiling of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, no crash reporters that phone home.

Contact & your rights

Questions, data requests, or deletions (trivial for us — the data lives only on your machine): ai@jazinski.com. We respond within two business days.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
