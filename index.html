FAQ

Does it actually keep Teams / Slack showing me as active?

Sashay posts genuine input events through macOS's event system — the same activity a chat app watches for. It's the same mechanism the "keep awake" utilities for remote workers use, with randomized timing so there's nothing mechanical about it.

Is this… allowed?

Sashay is a presence and keep-awake utility. Use it to hold your screen through long builds, readings, presentations, and monitoring shifts — not to deceive anyone with a genuine need to know you're away. Check your employer's policies; that's between you and them.
