How it works

Copy normally

Use ⌘C in any app. Stash silently captures it in the background.

Press ⌘⇧V

Your clipboard history appears. Type to search, or browse with arrow keys.

Copy or paste

Enter copies the selected item. ⌥Enter copies and pastes into your previous app.

FAQ

Does Stash sync to the cloud?

No. Stash makes zero network connections. Your clipboard history lives on your Mac and nowhere else.

Can I exclude my password manager?

Yes. Add any app to the exclusion list and Stash won't capture anything copied from it.

How is this different from Maccy?

Stash adds source-app tracking, pin support with visual indicators, ⌥N auto-paste, app exclusions with a UI (Maccy requires config file editing), and the same design system as the rest of the portfolio.
